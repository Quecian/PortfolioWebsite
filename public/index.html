Welcome to Palmetto Cosmetology Institute

This is Horry Georgetown Technical College's renowned spa, salon, and student training facility. Here, in-training students will get the real-world experience necessary to a successful career. Located on the Conway campus, buildings 500 & 600 will be home to Gator's Grotto, the top spot for hair care, Scales & Serenity, the first location for facials and face care, and Curated Claws, our newest nail salon. Our students will be under direct supervision of HGTC faculty, who are highly regarded practitioners of their professions, which you can see here.
